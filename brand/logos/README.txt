OUT AND ABOUT BOOKS: LOGO FILES

Main logo (stacked "OUT & ABOUT" with the Pride stripe)
  logo-stacked-colour       Charcoal lettering, pink "&", Pride stripe. Use on cream, white or light colours.
  logo-stacked-white        White lettering, yellow "&", Pride stripe. Use on pink, blue, charcoal or photos.
  logo-stacked-black        All black, solid bar. For one-colour printing, embroidery, stamps and engraving.
  logo-stacked-books-*      The same, with BOOKS under the stripe. Use where the name needs to be clear on its own:
                            the website, book spines, business cards, bookmarks.

Slogan ("Out. About. Unapologetic.")
  slogan-colour / slogan-white / slogan-black   Mainly for T-shirts, tote bags, posters and social media.

Icon ("OA" on a pink or charcoal square, with the Pride stripe)
  icon-oa-pink / icon-oa-charcoal   For the browser tab, phone home screens, social media profile pictures
                                    and very small uses, where the full logo would be too small to read.

File types
  .svg  Vector. Best for the website and for designers.
  .pdf  Vector. Send this to printers and merchandise suppliers (T-shirts, tote bags, bookmarks).
  .png  High resolution (3000 pixels wide) with a transparent background. For Word, Canva, social media.

Brand colours
  Hot pink   #D11F6B     Charcoal  #2B2F3A     Sunflower  #F7C948     Cobalt  #2F5FD0     Cream  #F6F2E9
  Pride stripe  #E40303  #FF8C00  #FFD200  #008026  #24408E  #732982
Typeface: Poppins Bold (free, SIL Open Font License). The logo lettering is converted to outlines,
so printers don't need the font installed.
